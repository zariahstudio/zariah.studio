Our Story

A Curated World
of Handbags

ZARIAH Studio is a Bangladesh-based multi-brand handbag destination. We bring together selected handbags from local and international brands so customers can explore a wider range of labels, styles and price points in one place.

From practical everyday bags to distinctive occasion styles, our selection is guided by variety, function and personal taste. Product information is presented to help customers compare options and choose with confidence.

Our catalogue features products from different brands. Brand names and product details are shown on individual listings where available, so you can understand what you are browsing before you order.

Our goal is to make discovering, comparing and ordering handbags straightforward, with helpful customer support whenever you need more information.

— ZARIAH Studio
