Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information about discovering, ordering and caring for your ZARIAH Studio bag. If you need more assistance, our team is one message away.

Orders & Shopping

How can I place an order?
You can explore the collections and contact us through the WhatsApp enquiry option on a product page. Our team will help confirm the product, availability and order details.
Can I order directly through WhatsApp?
Yes. Use the WhatsApp enquiry link on the product page to contact our team about the selected bag and complete your order conversation.
How do I know if a product is available?
Availability can vary by style and colour. Please contact our team with the product name or SKU for the latest availability confirmation before placing an order.
Can I ask about a different colour?
Yes. Contact us with the product name and preferred colour. Our team can confirm which options are currently available.

Payment & Delivery

What payment options are available?
Cash on delivery is offered on the storefront. Please confirm the available payment method and any applicable details with our team when placing your order.
Do you deliver across Bangladesh?
Delivery availability and charges depend on the destination. Share your delivery area with our team to confirm the available service and estimated delivery details.
How long does delivery take?
Delivery timing can vary by location and courier conditions. Our team will share the current estimated timeline when your order is confirmed.
Can I track my order?
For an order update, contact our team with your order details. We can share the latest status available for your order.

Products & Care

Where can I find product dimensions and details?
Open the individual product page to view its description, dimensions, style and available colour information. If anything is unclear, contact our team before ordering.
How should I care for my bag?
Keep your bag away from prolonged moisture, direct heat and harsh chemicals. Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth and store it in a clean, dry place when not in use.

Changes & Support

Can I change or cancel an order?
Please contact us as soon as possible with your order details. Whether a change or cancellation can be made depends on the order's current processing stage.
What is your return or exchange policy?
Please contact our team before sending an item back. We will confirm the applicable return or exchange terms for your order. The policy details should be confirmed with our team before purchase.
