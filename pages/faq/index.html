Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information about shopping handbags from local and international brands, placing orders, payments and delivery.

Brands & Products

Do you sell local and international handbag brands?
Yes. ZARIAH Studio brings together a curated selection of handbags from local and international brands. Explore the brand options shown in our catalogue.
How can I identify the brand of a bag?
Check the individual product page for the available brand and product information. If a brand detail is not shown or you need clarification, contact our team before ordering.
Where can I find product details?
Each product page shows the information available for that item, such as style, colour, dimensions, price and availability. Details can vary by product.
Are product origin details available?
We show origin information when it has been confirmed for the product. If the origin is not listed, please contact us for clarification before placing your order.

Orders & Payment

How can I place an order?
Choose a bag, add it to your shopping bag, proceed to checkout and submit your delivery details. You can contact our team if you need help before ordering.
What payment options are available?
Cash on Delivery is available for eligible orders. The available payment option and any applicable conditions will be confirmed during checkout or by our team.
Can I change or cancel an order?
Contact us as soon as possible with your order details. Whether a change or cancellation can be made depends on the order's processing stage.

Delivery & Support

Do you deliver across Bangladesh?
We offer delivery across Bangladesh. Delivery charges and estimated arrival times depend on your destination and the courier service.
How long does delivery take?
Delivery timing varies by destination and courier conditions. Our team will share the current estimated timeline when your order is confirmed.
Can I track my order?
Use the Track Order page with your order number and phone number to view the latest available update.
What is your return or exchange policy?
Please review our published policy before ordering. If you have a question about an item or an order, contact our team with the product or order details.

Still Have a Question?

Need help choosing between brands, checking product details or following up on an order? Our team is here to help.
