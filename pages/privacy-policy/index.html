Privacy Policy

Learn how ZARIAH Studio handles information when you browse our website, contact us or place an order.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ZARIAH Studio (“we”, “us” or “our”) may collect, use and protect information when you visit our website, contact us or place an order. ZARIAH Studio is a brand of InsightBridge.

2. Information We May Collect

  • Contact and order information: name, phone number, delivery address, order details and messages you choose to provide.
  • Enquiry information: product preferences, colour or availability requests and customer-support conversations.
  • Website and device information: browser type, device information, pages viewed, referring pages and approximate usage events.
  • Preference information: where enabled, wishlist or recently viewed items saved in your browser.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to respond to enquiries, confirm and fulfil orders, coordinate delivery, provide customer support, improve our website and understand how visitors use our services. We may also use information to protect the website, prevent misuse and maintain business records.

4. Cookies, Local Storage and Analytics

Our website may use browser storage to remember features such as wishlist or recently viewed products. We may use analytics or website measurement tools to understand site performance and visitor interactions. Your browser settings may allow you to restrict cookies or clear local storage; some site features may then work differently.

5. WhatsApp and Other Third-Party Services

If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram or another external service, your interaction is also subject to that provider’s privacy terms. We may use service providers for website hosting, analytics, database or order-related operations. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

6. When Information May Be Shared

We may share only relevant information with service providers involved in operating the website, processing an order, providing customer support or arranging delivery. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or to protect our legal rights. We do not offer customer personal information for sale.

7. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including order servicing, record keeping and legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no website, transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information you have provided, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. You can also clear browser storage through your device or browser settings. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our website is intended for a general shopping audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our website, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us through our Contact page or WhatsApp at +880 1642-779056.
