Our Story

A World of Bags.
One Destination.

ZARIAH Studio is a Bangladesh-based destination for handbags from a range of local and international brands. We bring different labels, styles and price points together so customers can discover more in one place.

From everyday essentials to occasion-ready styles, our selection is shaped around variety, practicality and personal taste. Product details and availability are presented to help you make an informed choice.

Whether you are exploring a local label or an international brand, ZARIAH Studio makes it easier to browse, compare and order handbags through one convenient shopping experience.
